# SJSU SafeMap: AI for Campus Safety Reporting and Awareness

Client: City of San José, campus safety  
Team: Taran Sahota, Rithvik, James, and Eric, Aug-Oct 2026  
My part: I helped build the interactive campus safety heat map and reporting interface.

## The problem

Students, staff, residents, and visitors may have difficulty understanding where safety concerns are reported around campus. Without a centralized visual system, it is harder to identify patterns and determine where additional attention may be needed.

## What we built

- An interactive campus map that displays reported safety activity by location and intensity.
- A reporting interface with separate options for SJSU students and staff, as well as residents and visitors.
- A prototype workflow where human analysts review reports, interpret the information, and decide what action to take.

## What I would do next

I would connect the prototype to a verified, privacy-protected incident database and test the reporting workflow with actual campus users.
